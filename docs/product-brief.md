# Warehouse Management & Logistics App

## Purpose

The Warehouse Management & Logistics App will help warehouse employees track inventory and manage warehouse activities from receiving goods through shipping them to customers.

## Target Process

1. Receiving — Record products when they arrive at the warehouse.
2. Putaway — Assign received products to the correct storage locations.
3. Cycle Counting — Regularly count inventory and compare it with system records.
4. Replenishment — Move additional stock to picking locations when inventory becomes low.
5. Picking — Collect the products required for an order.
6. Packing — Pack the selected products and prepare them for delivery.
7. Shipping — Record shipment details and update the order status.
8. Exception Handling — Record and resolve damaged, missing, incorrect, or delayed products.

## In Scope

- Recording received products
- Tracking inventory and storage locations
- Managing cycle counts
- Creating replenishment alerts
- Tracking picking and packing
- Updating shipment statuses
- Recording warehouse exceptions

## Out of Scope

- Customer billing and payment processing
- Employee payroll
- Vehicle and driver management
- Advanced delivery-route optimization
